# MiniSynth

A compact dual-oscillator synthesizer you can play in the browser — with live visuals that follow every note.

Play from the on-screen keyboard, your computer keys, or a MIDI controller. Shape the sound with classic synth controls, hear it with delay and reverb, and watch envelopes, filters, and waveforms move as you play.

## Sound

**Three oscillators** — Tabbed voices, each with pulse, saw, triangle, and sine. Set level and pitch per oscillator; pulse width appears when a voice is set to pulse. Stack them for detunes, intervals, and thicker tones.

**Pitch envelope** — Sweep pitch on each note with speed and amount controls, from subtle scoops to dramatic dives.

**Filter** — Sweep the cutoff from an initial to a final frequency, with control over how fast the move happens and how resonant it sounds. Great for plucks, sweeps, and evolving pads.

**Envelope (ADSR)** — Shape attack, decay, sustain, and release so notes can punch, bloom, or fade out gently.

**Vibrato** — Add motion with triangle or square modulation. Set rate, how long before it starts, how quickly it ramps in, and depth — from light shimmer to wide pitch swings.

## Effects

**Delay** — Echo with time, feedback, and mix.

**Reverb** — Space and wash with decay and mix.

**Master** — Overall volume, plus a live look at the resulting waveform.

## Live visuals

Each major section has its own display so you can see what you’re hearing:

- Oscillator waveform as the mix and shapes change
- Filter sweep over time
- Envelope shape with a playhead that tracks the current note
- Vibrato modulation curve
- Master output waveform

## Playing

- Click or tap the keyboard
- Use the computer keyboard (mapped like a piano row)
- Connect a MIDI keyboard or controller
- Shift octave with **Z** / **X**, or the on-screen octave buttons
- Transpose by semitone with **.** / **,**, or the transpose buttons
- Hit **Reset** anytime to return to the default patch

Polyphonic — hold chords, stack notes, and explore.
